WHY DO WE NEED POLE SURVEYS?

Utilities need records
they can act on.

A pole survey connects the installed equipment and its location to field observations. When photos and manual notes are scattered, that connection is hard to preserve and review.

01 / KNOW THE ASSET

Keep the inventory useful.

Teams need to know which poles, insulators and hardware are installed to plan upgrades and material requirements.

POLEPILOT HELPS

Component detections and structured survey suggestions give reviewers a starting point for the asset record.

02 / PLAN THE FOLLOW-UP

Give the next visit context.

Maintenance teams need evidence for deciding what to check on site. A photograph alone does not establish a verified fault or safe clearance.

POLEPILOT HELPS

See detections against the original image and save a human review note identifying what still needs verification.

03 / CONNECT FIELD & OFFICE

Keep evidence attached.

Office reviewers need a consistent record linking the photo, asset location and field assessment rather than disconnected images and notes.

POLEPILOT HELPS

Bring the image, review and supplied GPS coordinates together, then export JSON, CSV or a GeoJSON asset layer.

Where human verification matters

Model confidence is not calibrated accuracy. Occlusion, viewpoint and image quality affect results. Single-image clearance estimates are not survey-grade measurements, and do not certify electrical safety. Optional cloud interpretation is distinct from local detections.
